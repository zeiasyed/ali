Thinking Like a Geographer

Unit: Geography · How do maps help us learn about the Earth?

Roblox points bank 0 pts Get 90%+ on the quiz → earn 5 pts (once a day)

What are maps & globes?

  • A globe is a model of the Earth. It shows water, land, and countries.
  • You would travel about 25,000 miles to go around the Earth.
  • Maps are models of places. They show sizes, shapes, locations, and distances.
  • Maps can show roads, cities, national parks, landforms, boundaries, and weather.

Cartographers & map tools

  • A cartographer creates maps and decides what to include.
  • The title tells what the map is about.
  • The legend explains symbols and colors.
  • The compass rose shows cardinal directions: north, south, east, west.
  • The scale helps you tell distance (how far places are).

Kinds of maps

  • Population map — where people live
  • Political map — borders between cities, states, and countries
  • Physical map — natural features (mountains, valleys, lakes, streams)
  • Climate map — whether a place is usually warm, cold, wet, or dry
  • Climate is the study of weather over time
